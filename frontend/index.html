How a call
becomes a campaign

You do one thing: enroll. Everything after runs on the calls you were already getting.

01

Capture

Scam calls arrive on seeded lines Dossier controls and on the phones of people who enrolled. Both feed the same pipeline.

02

Classify

The agent answers every ambiguous call as a polite receptionist taking a message. Nothing else begins until the call is confirmed spam.

03

Elicit

A confused-but-cooperative caller keeps a scammer explaining. The agent never claims a false identity, never expresses interest in buying, never supplies payment details.

04

Record

Every call is recorded, disclosed plainly, and locked into a tamper-evident archive: write-once storage, cryptographic hashes, trusted timestamps.

05

Match

Script fingerprints, callback numbers, money routes and wave timing repeat across thousands of calls until they resolve into one named campaign.

06

Report

The impersonated brand gets the campaign, the evidence behind it, and an alert the moment a caller turns up holding one of their customers' credentials.

What the brand gets

Two standing alert layers, and a third that fires only when the evidence is unambiguous.

  • Campaign Intel — daily. The operation running in your name, mapped: script fingerprints, callback numbers, money routes, wave timing. This is the substrate everything else is built on.
  • Intercepts — per account. A caller holding one of your customers' credentials tries an OTP or a password on an enrolled phone. You get the alert while the account still exists, and you can verify it against your own auth logs.
  • Breach escalation — rare, and included. A caller recites internal-only data: an exact balance, yesterday's transaction. That is deterministic proof something leaked, from you or from a vendor. It is never priced by the incident.
  • Evidence, not screenshots. Every claim traces back to a recorded call in a write-once archive with a cryptographic hash and a trusted timestamp.
  • Referral packages when a campaign earns one. Operations that clear written, pre-committed criteria get a deep workup your team can hand to law enforcement. It is upside, never a promised rate.

Straight answers

Does this stop the calls?+

Yes. Dossier blocks spam calls on your line. They never ring through to you — our AI takes them instead, and turns what the caller says into a record.

Do you record the calls?+

Yes. Dossier's AI records every call it answers. It's disclosed in the terms you agree to when you sign up, and it's how the evidence is built.

Do I get paid for this?+

No. Dossier doesn't pay people to enroll and isn't an earning scheme. You're already getting these calls; this is what makes them count for something.

What do the brands see about me?+

Campaigns, not customers. What leaves Dossier is the operation: the script, the numbers, the routes. When a call impersonated your own bank or provider, you're told directly.

What do I actually have to do?+

Enroll with an email and a phone number, then finish connecting your number from the email we send. After that, the calls you already get do the work.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. Joining is free and you're never charged. The brands being impersonated are the customers.
