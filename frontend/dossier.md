Dossier

Sum up your business in one sentence.
Dossier is a brand-impersonation intelligence company, using AI agents to answer scam calls, extract identifying information, and then sell that as recurring intelligence and evidence packages to the major brands being impersonated.

What customer problem does your business solve or what unmet need does it address?
Americans lose billions of dollars a year to scam calls using the name of a brand the victim trusts. The brands being impersonated have no visibility into these calls: they can't see where the operations run from, what the script says, or where the money goes. By the time a brand finds out, their customer was already a victim.
The only way to see inside a scam call is to be on it, and no company can be on its customers' phones. Dossier can. Dossier answers scam calls in place of the victim, and reports back real-time intelligence on operations running live in the brand’s name.

Who is the customer with this problem? Describe your early adopter customer.
Dossier sells to major consumer brands that already fund teams fighting impersonation and still have no view into the phone channel, and it recruits high-volume scam-call recipients as the free sensor network that produces the intelligence those brands buy.




Please share your customer discovery progress. How has talking to potential customers influenced the design of your business?
For the past month we've been researching into the telemarketing space to find gaps that AI can fill. That has changed the business twice. Our original plan was to build TCPA claims to sue on behalf of consumers, but the legal exposure and timeline weren't something we could handle without a proper legal background. That led us to reframing the business model to focus on the real victim, the brand being impersonated, which needs no assignments and no litigation. It also changed what we were building. Everything in this space reacts to damage already done, so we moved to stopping the problem at the source, before it has cost anyone anything. We're early in discovery and haven't interviewed companies yet, so our next step is to speak with the fraud and brand protection teams who deal with these issues every day.


What alternatives do customers use today instead of your solution? Based on your early customer discovery, how do they currently solve this problem or address this need?
Today brands only find out they're being impersonated from a fraction of victims. When someone loses money and calls in, the brand pulls the number and files it for takedown. Amazon took down over 14,000 impersonation numbers last year that way, but this method is purely reactive and tells them nothing about who is behind the number. 
The tools built around these calls each get one part right. RoboKiller is a consumer app that blocks scam calls, protecting the user, but leaves valuable evidence on the table. Apate is the closest company to what we're building. They use AI agents against scammers for real-time intelligence, but they miss the most important piece of the puzzle: the customers. Apate operates solely on fake honeypot numbers and has stated they will never use a real consumer's phone. Because of this design decision, Apate never catches the scams that rely on user data, the ones that end with bank accounts drained. Even worse, banks on the Zelle network are now required to reimburse customers for exactly these losses, so every scam Apate misses is a check the bank has to write. Dossier is the only system that catches scammers before that check gets written. We answer the calls Apate never sees, on the phones where the money actually moves, and hand the brand what happened while there's still an account to save.
